
# Task name:

Evaluating the Persuasiveness of Debaters

# Task Introduction:
In this study, you will evaluate **persuasiveness in debate transcripts**. You will read transcripts from **two separate debates** on the same topic and judge which speaker is more persuasive.

Each debate features two participants, Speaker A and Speaker B. Although the debates involve different individuals, **Speaker A represents the same ideological or political position in both debates**.

Your task is to compare the persuasiveness of **Speaker A across the two debates**, focusing only on how effectively they argue their position.

There are **no right or wrong answers**! We are interested in your honest, subjective judgment based on the debate content.




# Task steps:
1. Review the **debate topic**, which is provided for context.
    
2. **Read Debate 1**, which includes Speaker A and Speaker B.
    
3. **Read Debate 2**, which includes a different Speaker A and Speaker B.
    
4. **Compare the two Speaker A participants** (one from each debate).
    
5. **Decide which Speaker A is more persuasive overall**, based solely on their arguments in the transcripts.
    
6. **Indicate how confident you are in your choice** using the following scale:
    - Very unsure
    - Somewhat unsure
    - Neutral
    - Somewhat confident
    - Very confident
        
7. Watch out for **attention checks** (see below).


    
**Important Notes **
